Lumen Reef 60 · control app, six styles

Every version is fully working and talks to the same simulated light (mock API), so settings you change in one show up in the others after a reload. PAR values are estimates; no hardware exists yet.

  1. 1 · AmbientThe whole screen is lit by the light. A day ribbon paints 24 hours in the light's own colors; drag it to preview any time.
  2. 2 · AluminiumMachined product feel. Look up into the fixture through a lens and turn its ring to set master brightness.
  3. 3 · SwissStrict grid, huge numerals, a solid block of the current light color. Bar-chart sliders and 24 hour cells.
  4. 4 · ConsoleLighting desk: a fader per LED channel with live LED meters, an LCD PAR readout and scene buttons.
  5. 5 · AlmanacA 24 h sun clock on sea-chart paper. Drag the sun around the dial; the ring shows the light's color and strength.
  6. 6 · SpectrometerLab instrument. The emission spectrum is the hero, with each channel's fader standing under its own wavelength.
